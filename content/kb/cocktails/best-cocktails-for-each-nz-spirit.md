New Zealand's craft distilling scene leans heavily toward gin, with growing whisky, vodka and rum categories (see "What spirits are New Zealand distilleries best known for?"). Here's how to make the most of each at home.

## Gin

Gin's versatility means it works across a huge range of cocktails, but a few classics really let a good gin shine:

- **Gin and Tonic** — simple, and the best way to actually taste a gin's botanicals against a mixer.
- **Martini** — the ultimate test of a clean, well-balanced gin.
- **Negroni** — bold, bittersweet, and forgiving of a more assertive, juniper-heavy gin (see our dedicated Negroni recipe guide).

## Whisky

- **Old Fashioned** — arguably the best showcase for a good single malt or blended whisky (see our dedicated recipe guide).
- **Whisky Sour** — balances whisky's richness with citrus and sweetness, a great option for a bolder or higher-proof pour.
- **Highball** — whisky and soda over ice; simple, refreshing, and a great match for a smoother, less peaty style.

## Vodka

- **Espresso Martini** — vodka's clean neutrality lets coffee and coffee liqueur lead (see our dedicated recipe guide).
- **Vodka Soda / Vodka Tonic** — a clean, low-intervention way to enjoy vodka's texture without competing flavours.
- **Moscow Mule** — vodka, ginger beer and lime; a classic long drink that suits a smooth, well-filtered vodka.

## Rum

- **Daiquiri** — a simple, classic showcase for a clean white rum.
- **Dark 'n' Stormy** — dark or aged rum with ginger beer, a great match for a rum with real barrel character.
- **Mojito** — white rum, lime, mint and soda; refreshing and forgiving.
