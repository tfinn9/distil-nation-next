The Espresso Martini is a relatively modern classic (created in the 1980s), built around vodka's clean neutrality letting real coffee flavour lead.

## Classic recipe

- **50ml vodka**
- **20ml coffee liqueur**
- **25ml freshly brewed espresso**, cooled slightly
- **5–10ml simple syrup** (adjust to taste and to how sweet your coffee liqueur already is)
- Coffee beans, for garnish

## Method

1. Add all ingredients to a cocktail shaker with plenty of ice.
2. **Shake hard** for a good 15–20 seconds — this drink relies on vigorous shaking to build its characteristic frothy top layer, so don't hold back.
3. Double-strain into a chilled Martini or coupe glass (see "What ice should you use for cocktails?" and our cocktail tools guide for why double-straining gives a cleaner pour).
4. Garnish with a few whole coffee beans on top of the foam.

## Getting the froth right

The signature foam on top comes from vigorous shaking combined with the natural oils and proteins in freshly made espresso (pre-made, older, or instant coffee generally won't froth as well) — freshly pulled espresso, even a small amount, makes a real difference to the final look and texture.

## Vodka choice

Because vodka's role here is mostly textural and as a clean alcohol base, a solid, smooth vodka is all you need — this is a good drink to use a mid-range bottle in rather than your most expensive one, since the coffee and liqueur do most of the flavour work.
