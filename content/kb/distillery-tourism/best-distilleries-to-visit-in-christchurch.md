*Based on the Distil-Nation NZ directory. Confirm current opening days, booking requirements and exact locations directly with each distillery before visiting — some entries below are in wider Canterbury rather than central Christchurch.*

Canterbury has one of the largest clusters of distilleries in the country in our directory (14 total), including several set up for visitors.

## Distilleries with a cellar door or tours (Canterbury region)

- **The Spirits Workshop** — Christchurch-based; cellar door and tours available.
- **Akaroa Craft Distillery** — cellar door and tours available; note this is on Banks Peninsula in Akaroa, roughly an hour's drive from central Christchurch, not within the city itself.
- **Humdinger Gin** — cellar door available.
- **4 Bars Distillery** — cellar door available.
- **Southern Grain Spirits (NZ) Ltd (Kaiapoi Distillery)** — cellar door available; located in Kaiapoi, just north of Christchurch city.

## Planning a visit

Because a few of these are outside the immediate city centre (Akaroa in particular is a distinct day-trip destination on Banks Peninsula), it's worth deciding whether you want a compact city-based visit or a wider day trip around greater Canterbury, and planning accordingly.

## Before you go

Contact each distillery directly to confirm current tour/tasting availability, opening hours and whether bookings are required — cellar door hours for small producers can change, and this list reflects our directory at time of writing rather than a live, constantly updated booking system.
