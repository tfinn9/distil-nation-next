Wellington has 7 distilleries in our directory — a smaller scene than Auckland or Canterbury, but with a few genuinely visitor-friendly options.

## Distilleries with a cellar door (Wellington region)

- **Southward Distilling** — cellar door available.
- **Ranui Essentials** — cellar door available.
- **The Bond Store** — cellar door available.

None of Wellington's listed distilleries currently show public tours in our directory (as opposed to a cellar door/tasting offering), so it's worth contacting ahead if a guided tour specifically is what you're after, rather than assuming one is available.

## Planning a visit

Wellington's compact size makes it easy to combine a distillery cellar door visit with the city's broader food, wine and craft beer scene in a single day — worth checking opening days in advance, since small producers' cellar doors are often not open every day of the week.

## Before you go

Contact each distillery directly to confirm current opening hours, tasting availability and whether bookings are required — this reflects our directory at time of writing, not a live booking system.
