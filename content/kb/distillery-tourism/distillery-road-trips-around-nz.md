*This article is a general framework, not a verified turn-by-turn itinerary. Specific opening days, drive times and current operating status need to be checked distillery-by-distillery before planning a real trip — see our Distillery Tourism caveats elsewhere in this section.*

Because craft distilleries are spread across so many New Zealand regions, several natural road-trip routes suggest themselves — though we'd want to verify current opening days and confirm exact locations before publishing a fully mapped itinerary.

## Otago / Central Otago and Waitaki

Given the concentration of whisky-focused producers in this wider region (Cardrona Distillery near Wānaka, the New Zealand Whisky Collection near Ōamaru, among others — see our Dunedin guide for caveats on exact locations), a South Island high-country loop covering Wānaka, Cromwell/Central Otago and Ōamaru is a promising route on paper, but needs confirmed distances and opening days before being published as a set itinerary.

## Canterbury and Banks Peninsula

Combining Christchurch-based producers with a detour to Akaroa on Banks Peninsula (home to Akaroa Craft Distillery) could make for a strong one-to-two-day loop — see "The best distilleries to visit in Christchurch" for the specific names involved.

## Auckland and the Hauraki Gulf

A route combining mainland Auckland distilleries with a Waiheke Island ferry crossing (home to Waiheke Distilling Co. and Waiheke Whisky) offers a strong city-plus-island contrast — see "The best distilleries to visit in Auckland."

## What's needed to publish a fully mapped version

To turn this into a genuinely useful road-trip guide (with drive times, suggested day-by-day stops, and confirmed opening days), we'd need to verify each distillery's exact address and current visitor status — much of this data isn't yet confirmed at the town/city level in our directory (see `data/nz-distilleries.md`, which explicitly flags this as unverified). This is a strong candidate for a future, more deeply researched follow-up article.
