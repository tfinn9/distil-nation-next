*Based on the Distil-Nation NZ directory at time of writing. Always confirm current tasting availability directly with the distillery.*

Of the 107 distilleries currently tracked in our directory, **39** list a cellar door or visitor centre, which generally indicates tastings are available on-site (either included or for a fee), and **20** specifically list guided tours (which usually include a tasting as part of the experience).

## How to find one

The best way to find a distillery offering tastings near you is to browse our [Distilleries](/distilleries/) directory and filter by region — see our regional guides (Christchurch, Auckland, Wellington, and Dunedin, though the Dunedin guide currently carries significant caveats about location accuracy) for specific named examples.

## What to expect from a tasting

Tasting formats vary by producer — some offer a simple flight of their core range for a set price, others structure tastings around food pairings or guided walkthroughs of their range from lightest to most intense. Because these details vary distillery to distillery and change over time, it's best to check directly with a specific producer rather than assume a standard format.

## Not all "By appointment" listings mean no tastings

A number of distilleries in our directory are marked "by appointment" rather than having a fixed cellar door — this doesn't mean tastings aren't available, just that you'll need to contact the distillery directly to arrange a visit rather than simply dropping in.
