*Important caveat: our directory only records a broad "Otago" region, not individual towns, and Otago covers a large area including Dunedin, Wānaka, Central Otago and the Waitaki Valley. This article should be treated as a starting point only — we have not independently verified which of the following are actually within Dunedin city versus elsewhere in the wider Otago region, and some names below are known (from general knowledge, not directory data) to be located outside Dunedin entirely.*

## What we can say with reasonable confidence

- **Cardrona Distillery** is located near **Wānaka**, not Dunedin — despite being tagged "Otago" in our directory.
- **The New Zealand Whisky Collection** is associated with **Ōamaru**, in the Waitaki district — also tagged "Otago" in our directory, but not Dunedin.

## Names that may genuinely be in or near Dunedin (unverified)

Based on their names alone — which is not a reliable substitute for verification — the following *may* be located in or close to Dunedin, but this needs to be confirmed directly with each producer before being published as a travel guide:

- **Saddle Hill Distillery** — Saddle Hill is a real Dunedin suburb, so this is plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Sandymount Distillery** — Sandymount is a locality on the Otago Peninsula near Dunedin, so this is plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Dunedin Craft Spirits** — the name strongly suggests a Dunedin base, but exact address/visitor details are unconfirmed.

## What we're not confident about

**Lammermoor Distillery**, **Rifters**, and **No8 Distillery** are all tagged "Otago" in our directory, but we don't have reliable information on whether any of these are actually within Dunedin city limits versus elsewhere in the wider region.
