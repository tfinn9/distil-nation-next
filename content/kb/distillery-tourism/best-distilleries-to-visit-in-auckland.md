Auckland leads the country with 29 distilleries in our directory — the largest single-region concentration by a wide margin.

## Distilleries with a cellar door or tours (Auckland region)

- **1919 Distilling** — tours available.
- **Kākāpō Distillery** — cellar door available.
- **Waiheke Distilling Co.** — cellar door and tours available; located on Waiheke Island, reached by ferry from downtown Auckland.
- **Waiheke Whisky** — cellar door and tours available; also on Waiheke Island.
- **Coromandel Distilling Company (Awildian Gin)** — cellar door available.
- **Carbon6ix** — cellar door available.
- **Lavender Hill Distillery** — cellar door and tours available.
- **Sip Distilling Co** — cellar door available.
- **NZ Rum Co.** — cellar door and tours available.

## Mainland Auckland vs Waiheke Island

Worth noting when planning a day: several of the region's most visitor-friendly distilleries (Waiheke Distilling Co. and Waiheke Whisky) are on **Waiheke Island**, which requires a ferry trip from central Auckland — a great day-trip option, but not a quick drop-in if you're staying in the city itself. The rest of the list above are on the Auckland mainland.

## Before you go

Contact each distillery directly to confirm current tour/tasting availability, opening hours and whether bookings are required — this reflects our directory at time of writing, not a live booking system.
