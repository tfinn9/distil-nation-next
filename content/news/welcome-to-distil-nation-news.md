Distil-Nation NZ has always been about the podcast — Tom, Cameron & Ty chatting to distillers across the country. But there's a lot happening in New Zealand's craft spirits scene between episodes, and it doesn't always fit into a podcast format.

That's what this section is for.

Going forward, we'll be using this space to cover:

* New distillery openings and closures
* Product launches and limited releases
* Awards and competition results
* Regulatory changes affecting New Zealand producers
* Industry events, festivals, and tastings
* Notable happenings from the wider NZ spirits community

If you're a distillery with news to share, get in touch via our [contact page](/contact/) — we'd love to hear from you.

Stay tuned.
